Bad River Band of the Lake Superior Chippewa Indians Res. No. 09-02-26-09. Changes to Chapter 3.02 - Hunting of Deer

WHEREAS, the Bad River Band of Lake Superior Tribe of Chippewa Indians is a federally recognized Indian Tribe with a Constitution enacted pursuant to the Indian Reorganization Act of 1934, 25 U.S.C. Section 476; and

WHEREAS, Article VI Section 1(n) of the Constitution directs the Tribal Council to encourage and foster the arts, crafts, traditions, culture, wildlife, and natural resources of the Band; and

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the Tribal Council hereby approves the following changes to Chapter 3.02 - Hunting of Deer:

1. In Chapter 3.02 change the word deer to the words big game; and

2. In Section 3.02.020 add the definition:

(g) Big Game: deer, bear, and elk

CERTIFICATION

I, the undersigned, as Secretary of the Bad River Band of Lake Superior Chippewa Indians of Wisconsin, hereby certify that the Tribal Council is composed of 7 members, of whom 7, constituting a quorum, were present at a meeting duly called, noticed, and held on the 2 day of September, 2026, that the foregoing resolution was duly adopted at such meeting by a vote of 6 for, 0 against, and 0 abstaining.

_________________________

Laura Miranda, Tribal Secretary
